what it does

three passes between your click and the frame.

  1. 01

    reads the press

    stages scan your timing ahead of the input, before the frame is drawn.

  2. 02

    picks the earliest frame

    the press is held and matched to the earliest frame that still clears.

  3. 03

    sends it on time

    your early click lands where it should, without changing how you play.
